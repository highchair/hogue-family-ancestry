## About

M. Genevieve is the seventh child, born soon after her brother Jean dies at just one month old. She marries at age 17 and has her first child four years later. She dies young, just a month shy of her 30th birthday, a few days after her last son is born. He only lives to be five months old.

Augustin remarries almost exactly a year after M. Genevieve’s death (one year and 10 days).


## Notes

+ **Augustin remarries** – Augustin remarries to Francoise Labelle on 25 November, 1748. (Generation 11.32) Marcel did not pursue this second marriage since children would have the Gaulin surname.
+ Typically, the location of Canada is shown for familiarity purposes. At this time in Canadian history, it would have been known as “Nouvelle France” (New France). See (Generation 11.32) Marcel’s “Introduction” and “Story of Pierre” for additional information. _New France became the British Province of Quebec in 1763 after France ceded it in the Treaty of Paris at the end of the Seven Years’ War._
